CONNECT YOUTUBE (official login) - one-time setup, about 10 minutes
===================================================================

Why: with the official login the app can see your private videos without cookies,
and it never expires (once your Google app is published to production).

1. Go to https://console.cloud.google.com and sign in with the Google account that owns your channel.
2. Create a NEW PROJECT (any name).
3. APIs & Services -> Library -> search "YouTube Data API v3" -> Enable.
4. Google Auth Platform (OAuth consent screen):
   - Audience: External.
   - Fill in an app name and your email, accept the policy.
   - While the project is in "Testing", add your own Google account under Audience -> Test users
     (the login then expires every 7 days).
   - To avoid the 7-day expiry, publish the app to production ("Publish app"). Google then asks
     for a homepage and a privacy policy URL; a simple free GitHub Pages page is enough.
5. Clients -> Create client -> Application type: Desktop app -> Create -> Download JSON.
6. Rename the downloaded file to client_secret.json and put it in the app folder.
7. In the app: Settings -> Connect YouTube. The browser opens: choose your account and channel.
   "Google hasn't verified this app" is normal (it is YOUR app): Advanced -> Go to (app) -> Continue -> Allow.

The app only asks for what it needs (the "youtube" permission to switch private videos to unlisted).
client_secret.json and youtube_token.json are SECRETS: never share or upload them.
To remove access any time: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
